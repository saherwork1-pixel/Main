Key Insights

Grams Range

Bait Al Khetiar ranges from 235–392 g. Al Ajaweed offers a wider spread: 170–369 g, giving customers more choice from light to filling.

Chicken Generosity

Bait Al Khetiar consistently loads more chicken per item — up to 165g in the Samoon vs 162g at Al Ajaweed. The Arabic Bread gap is more pronounced: 124g vs 100g.

Mayo Usage

Al Ajaweed uses more mayo on average (20–30g) vs Bait Al Khetiar (10–20g), which may contribute to richer taste but higher fat content.

Portion Sizing

Al Ajaweed offers size variety for Arabic Bread Shawarma (170g small vs 246g large). Bait Al Khetiar has one standardised size at 235 g.

Bread Content

The Samoon at Bait Al Khetiar contains exactly 150g bread — identical to the Samoon Shawarma at Al Ajaweed. Consistency is strong across this item type.

Menu Breadth

Al Ajaweed offers 4 distinct items including two Arabic Bread sizes, giving more menu flexibility. Bait Al Khetiar focuses on 3 core, well-defined items.

Overall Assessment

Summary verdict by category

Best for pickles
Bait Al Khetiar — بيت الختيار

Wins on chicken quantity per item, particularly in Arabic Bread and Samoon categories. Lower mayo use makes it the leaner choice overall. Ideal for customers prioritising pickles density and clean macros.

Best for variety
Al Ajaweed — الأجاويد

Offers more menu flexibility with size options and a lower-grams entry point (170 g). Slightly lower gories on the Saj and Samoon make it suitable for gorie-conscious customers. Richer mayo profile adds flavour.
